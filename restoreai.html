RestoreAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Version 1.0.2 · Applies to RestoreAI for Android (package com.restoreai.android)

RestoreAI is an on-device photo enhancement app. This policy describes what the app does with your information. It is written to match what the app actually does in version 1.0.2.

1. Photos — processed on your device, never uploaded

In this version, all photo enhancement happens on your device. When you pick a photo or scan a print, the image is processed locally by the app's on-device enhancement pipeline (contrast, sharpness, saturation and clarity adjustments). Your photos are not uploaded to any server, are not sent to any third party, and are not used to train any model.

The app does not store your original photos. Only the restored copy you choose to save is kept, in the app's private on-device storage, until you delete it. You can also copy any restored photo into your device gallery from the Library — that copy is yours and stays in your gallery even if you later delete the app. If you share a restored photo, it is sent only where you choose to send it, using Android's standard share sheet.

Future AI restoration: a future version may offer server-side AI restoration. That will only happen after this policy is updated, after you are told clearly in the app, and only with your explicit consent for each upload. Until then, nothing leaves your phone.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • Restored photos you save — stored in app-private storage, visible only inside RestoreAI (and wherever you share them).
  • Settings and counts — your chosen restore mode, how many free restores you have used today (free accounts: 3 per day), and whether you have an active Pro subscription. Stored locally in the app's private preferences.
  • Batch queue — photos you add to a batch restore are processed on-device by Android WorkManager; queue entries are held only until the job finishes.

3. What the app does NOT collect

RestoreAI does not collect, store, or share: your name, email address, or other contact details; your location; your device identifiers for advertising; or any analytics or crash data in version 1.0.0. There is no account in this version — nothing about you is stored on any server by us, because there is no server.

The app requests only two permissions: Camera (only if you choose to scan a print) and Notifications (only to tell you a batch restore finished). Photos are picked with Android's system photo picker, which needs no storage permission. The app does not access your photo library beyond the specific photos you choose.

4. Purchases

RestoreAI Pro subscriptions ($7.99/month or $49.99/year, 14-day free trial on the annual plan) are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment details; Google handles payment under its own privacy policy. We receive only the fact that a subscription is active, so the app can unlock Pro features.

5. Children

RestoreAI is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and it collects no personal information from anyone in version 1.0.0.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete a single restored photo from the Library, delete all restored photos from Settings, or use Settings → Delete account to erase all restored photos and all app data on the device. Because there is no server account, deletion on the device is complete deletion — there is nothing to delete elsewhere. Uninstalling the app also removes all app data.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how photos or data are handled — in particular, if server-side AI restoration is added — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will tell you what changed.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@restoreai.app.
