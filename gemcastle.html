Gem Castle — Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 November 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · Applies to Gem Castle (package com.gemcastle.android)

Short version

Gem Castle collects no personal data. You do not need an account to play. Your progress stays on your device. There are no ads and no analytics software in the app. Purchases are handled by Google Play, and we never see your payment details.

Data we collect

None. Gem Castle does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. We operate no server for this app in version 1.0.0.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, so the game works offline:

  • Level progress, stars earned, and best scores
  • Castle renovation progress
  • Lives and refill timing
  • Booster inventory and daily-reward claim time
  • Season Pass status (as reported by Google Play)
  • Your settings: sound, music, notifications, and the display name you choose

None of this leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it from inside the app at any time: Settings → Account → Delete my data.

Advertising

Gem Castle shows no advertisements and includes no advertising software development kit (SDK). No advertising identifier is collected.

Analytics

Version 1.0.0 includes no analytics SDK. We do not track how you play.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (extra lives, moves, boosters, and the Season Pass subscription) are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase succeeded, so the game can grant what you bought. We never receive your card details, billing address, or Google account credentials. Subscription management, cancellation, and refunds are handled through Google Play.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app sends exactly one kind: a reminder when your lives have refilled. No other notifications are sent. You can turn this off in Settings → Notifications, or in your device settings.

Children

Gem Castle is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 as a primary audience, and it collects no data from anyone, including children. Purchases require the device owner's Google Play approval.

Support contact

If you email support (support@gemcastle.app), we use your message only to answer you, and we keep it only as long as needed to resolve your request. We do not share it with anyone.

Your choices

  • Play without an account: no account is needed, ever.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Account → Delete my data erases all local progress, settings, and state from your device.
  • Notifications: on or off, in Settings or in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds any data collection (for example, an analytics tool or an account system), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@gemcastle.app
