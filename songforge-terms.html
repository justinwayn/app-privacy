What SongForge is

SongForge turns details and lyrics you provide into a song for one person, using AI. There is no account and no sign-in; the app and everything you make in it live on your device.

Preview status

SongForge is still being finished. In this version, generation produces a short preview melody with your lyrics timed to it — not the full sung song. The app says so on the screens where it matters. Your words, drafts, and library carry over when the full song service launches.

Free songs and credits

  • Every install includes 3 free songs. After that, making a song costs 1 credit.
  • SongForge Pro includes 50 credits per month. Unused credits do not roll over.
  • If a generation fails, the credit is returned automatically.

Rules that protect people
