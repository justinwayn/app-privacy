Privacy Policy — Chip

Last updated: 14 November 2026  ·  App: Chip (com.chiprecovery.app), version 1.0.0

Chip is a recovery companion pet app. This policy explains what Chip does — and does not do — with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone. Nothing leaves the device.

1. What we collect

We collect nothing. Chip has no account system, no sign-in, no server, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK. There is no company server that receives your data, because there is no company server.

What you enter in the app — daily check-ins (mood, optional craving level, optional journal line), slip logs, meeting logs, milestone chips, your pet's name, and reminder settings — is stored only in a local database on your phone (Room) and in local app preferences (DataStore).

2. What leaves your phone

Nothing. Chip makes no network requests of its own. The app does not request the INTERNET permission. The only outside service involved is Google Play itself, when you choose to buy Chip Pro: that purchase is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy terms, and Chip never sees your payment details.

3. Purchases

Chip Pro is an optional subscription sold through Google Play Billing: $7.99 per month, or $49.99 per year with a 14-day free trial on the annual plan. Google Play handles the payment, trial, cancellation, and refund process. Chip stores only whether Google Play reports an active Pro subscription, so it can unlock the Pro extras (extra pet customisations, unlimited meeting log history, milestone keepsakes). No payment information is stored in the app.

4. Permissions

Chip asks for one permission, and only in context:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — only if you turn on the daily reminder in Settings. The reminder is one gentle nudge a day ("Your pet is thinking of you"). It is never asked for at first launch, and you can turn it off at any time.

Chip does not ask for location, contacts, storage, camera, or microphone access.

5. Data export and deletion

You can export your check-ins, meetings, and chips as a CSV file from Settings; the file is saved to your phone's Downloads folder and goes nowhere else. You can also delete all your Chip data from Settings ("Delete my data") — this erases the local database and preferences completely, on the phone, immediately. Deleting the app also deletes all its data. There is no copy anywhere else to delete, because there is no copy anywhere else.

6. Children

Chip is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Health information

Chip is a companion for self-reported streaks. It is not medical treatment and does not treat, cure, or prevent addiction. It does not give medical advice, dosage advice, or diagnoses. The support resources in the app (including the SAMHSA National Helpline, 1-800-662-4357, and the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) are static informational text. If you are struggling, please contact a healthcare professional, a sponsor, or one of those helplines.

8. Changes to this policy

If Chip ever changes how it handles data — for example, if a future version adds optional backup — this policy will be updated here first, and the app will ask you before any data leaves the phone. Version 1.0.0 does not send data anywhere, and that will not change silently.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the "Ask about gifting" / support path inside the app, or write to the support contact listed on the Play Store listing. (A dedicated support email address is being set up and will be added here before the closed test.)
