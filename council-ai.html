Privacy Policy — Council AI

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Version 1.0.2

Council AI ("the app") is an AI chat assistant built to ask GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Grok the same question and show their answers side by side, with a "Best answer" combined mode. Version 1.0.2 is a preview edition: it shows sample answers in each model's voice while the live council is finished. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

1. What the app stores on your device

Your chats, messages, projects (folders), preferences (default answer mode, default model, usage counters), and an optional email address are stored locally on your device using Android Room and DataStore. They are not uploaded to us. Saved chats remain readable offline, and any chat can be exported as text from inside the app.

2. What happens when you ask a question

In this preview build, nothing leaves your device when you ask a question. The answers are samples, produced on the device by the app's built-in preview provider and labelled "Sample answer" in the app. The app does not connect to any answer server in this build, and it never contains an API key. When the live council opens — answers sent to a service that asks the real models — this policy will be updated and the date above changed before that build ships (see section 8).

The app does not send your question to Google, OpenAI, xAI, or any model provider directly.

3. Voice input and photos

Voice input uses Android's built-in speech recognizer; audio is processed by the system service, not by us. Photos use the system photo picker — the app receives only the photo you choose and requests no broad storage permission. In this preview build, attached photos are not read or analyzed; the app tells you so on screen when you attach one.

4. Purchases

Pro subscriptions ($12.99/month or $79.99/year, 14-day free trial on annual) are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. Google Play shares only the fact that a subscription is active, so the app can unlock Pro features and enforce the higher usage caps.

5. What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no ads, and no ad tracking.
  • No location data, contacts, or device identifiers beyond what Android itself requires for the app to run.
  • No analytics or crash data leaves the device in this version. Crash reporting is local logging only; a crash-reporting service will be added before the first closed test and this policy will be updated to name it before then.
  • No API keys or credentials are stored in the app.

6. Data retention and deletion

Chats stay on your device until you delete them (from the chat list) or delete your account. Settings > Delete account & all data permanently deletes every chat, message, project, and saved preference on the device. Deleting your account in the app does not cancel a Pro subscription — cancel that separately in Google Play.

7. Children

Council AI is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If the app's data practices change — for example when live answers or crash reporting are added — this policy will be updated here and the "Last updated" date changed before the change ships.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@councilai.app
