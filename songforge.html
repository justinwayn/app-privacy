SongForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Applies to: SongForge for Android, version 1.0.0.

SongForge ("we") makes custom songs with vocals from your prompts and lyrics. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it — in plain language.

What we collect — and why

  • Email address. You sign in with your email and a one-time code. We use it to run your account and your credits. We do not store passwords.
  • Prompts, template details, and lyrics. The details you type (including a recipient's first name) and any lyrics you write are used to draft and generate your song. In the current version (1.0.0) this data is processed and stored on your device. When the SongForge service backend launches, generated songs will be stored server-side so they survive a new phone; this policy will be updated before that release, and the Data Safety section will be updated in the same release.
  • Generated audio. Your finished songs are stored on your device so you can play them offline. Deleting a song removes its audio from the device.
  • Optional cover photo. Only if you pick one for a share video, using the system photo picker. The photo is used only as that video's cover. We never ask for broad photo or storage access.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card numbers. We receive purchase confirmations from Google Play so we can grant your credits.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. Only from software we declare: in version 1.0.0, crashes are logged locally on your device only. Crashlytics will be added from the first closed-test build and will be named here and in the Data Safety section when it ships.

What we do NOT collect

  • No microphone audio. There is no recording feature in SongForge, and the app requests no microphone permission.
  • No precise location, no contacts, no health data, no financial account data, no browsing history.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no advertising SDKs in the app, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

What we share

In version 1.0.0, nothing is shared with third parties: your songs and account data stay on your device, and purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. When the service backend launches, generated songs will be processed by our music-generation provider under its written terms; the provider will be named here before that release.

How long we keep things

  • Deleted songs: audio is removed from the device immediately on deletion.
  • Rejected prompts (for example, a request in a specific artist's style): the prompt text is not retained. We keep only a hash and the rule that was hit, so we can audit the filter.
  • Deleted accounts: your account, library, and stored songs are deleted when you delete your account in Settings. A Google Play subscription is cancelled separately in Play → Subscriptions — deleting your account here does not cancel it silently, and the app tells you so in the deletion flow.

Your choices

  • Download your songs: Settings → My data, or download each song from its player screen.
  • Notifications: Settings → Notifications — generation-complete alerts, on or off.
  • Clear downloads: Settings → Storage — removes downloaded audio from the device.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. This is a real deletion, not a deactivation.
  • Report a song / takedown: every song has a Report action in the app, or email support@songforge.app with the song or link, your contact details, and the basis of your claim. Clear copyright claims by the rights holder disable the share link within 48 hours of a complete claim while we review. A public web takedown form will be linked here before production.

Children

SongForge is for adults (18+). The "Kid's theme song" template is for an adult to make a song for a child — it collects only a first name the adult provides, creates no child accounts, and asks for no child photos. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

AI disclosure

Songs are generated by AI. Share cards carry a "Made with AI" disclosure, and songs are never presented as a real artist's work. Songs cannot be requested in a specific artist's style.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes and the new version is posted here before the change takes effect in a release.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@songforge.app.
