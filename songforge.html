SongForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Applies to: SongForge for Android, version 1.0.2.

SongForge turns your words into a song for one person. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it — in plain language.

The short version

SongForge has no account and no sign-in. Nothing you type, write, or make is collected on a server — it all stays on your phone. The only outside party involved is Google Play, which handles subscriptions.

What stays on your device

  • Template details and lyrics. The details you type (including a recipient's first name) and any lyrics you write are stored on your device and used to draft your song. They are never uploaded.
  • Generated audio. Your songs are stored on your device so you can play them offline. Deleting a song removes its audio from the device.
  • Credits and settings. Your credit balance and preferences live in the app's storage on your device.
  • Optional cover photo. Only if you pick one for a share card, using the system photo picker. The photo is used only as that card's cover. We never ask for broad photo or storage access.

What Google Play handles

  • Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card numbers. The app receives purchase confirmations from Google Play so it can grant your credits.

What we do NOT collect

  • No email address, no account, no password — there is nothing to sign in to.
  • No microphone audio. There is no recording feature in SongForge, and the app requests no microphone permission.
  • No precise location, no contacts, no health data, no financial account data, no browsing history.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no advertising SDKs in the app, and we do not sell your data to anyone.
  • No crash reporting leaves the device in this version. If crash reporting is added in a later release, it will be named here and in the Play Data Safety section in the same release.

Preview status

SongForge is still being finished. In this version, what you hear is a short preview melody with your lyrics timed to it — not the full sung song. Your words, drafts, and library are stored on your device and carry over when the full song service launches. This policy will be updated before that release, and the Data Safety section will be updated in the same release.

How long we keep things

  • Deleted songs: audio is removed from the device immediately on deletion.
  • Rejected prompts (for example, a request in a specific artist's style): the prompt text is not retained. The app keeps only a hash and the rule that was hit, so the filter can be audited.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account removes your library, drafts, credits, and stored songs from the device. A Google Play subscription is cancelled separately in Play → Subscriptions — deleting here does not cancel it, and the app tells you so in the deletion flow.

Your choices

  • Download your songs: Settings → My data → Save all songs to this device, or download each song from its player screen.
  • Notifications: Settings → Notifications — song-ready alerts, on or off.
  • Clear downloads: Settings → Storage — removes downloaded audio from the device.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account. This is a real deletion, not a deactivation.
  • Report a song / takedown: every song has a Report action in the app, or email support@songforge.app with the song or link, your contact details, and the basis of your claim. Clear copyright claims by the rights holder disable the share link within 48 hours of a complete claim while we review.

Children

SongForge is for adults (18+). The "Kid's theme song" template is for an adult to make a song for a child — it collects only a first name the adult provides, creates no child accounts, and asks for no child photos. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

AI disclosure

Songs are generated by AI. Share cards carry a "Made with AI" disclosure, and songs are never presented as a real artist's work. Songs cannot be requested in a specific artist's style.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes and the new version is posted here before the change takes effect in a release.

Contact
