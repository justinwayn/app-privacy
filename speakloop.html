SpeakLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Version: 1.0.0

SpeakLoop ("the app") is a language-learning app by Justin Wayn. This policy describes what the app collects, what it does not collect, and your choices. If you have questions, email support@speakloop.app.

1. What the app collects — and where it lives

On your device only (V1): your learning progress (streak, XP, completed scenarios), your vocabulary word bank, your conversation-minute usage, and your settings (learning direction, voice speed, reminder preference). This data is stored in on-device storage (Room database and DataStore) and is never sent to our servers — in V1 there is no SpeakLoop server account at all.

Voice input: when you speak during a conversation, your voice is processed on your device by Android's speech recognition (on-device where available) and by Android TextToSpeech for the partner voice. No audio is recorded, stored, or uploaded by SpeakLoop in V1. The in-app conversation partner in V1 is a scripted mock provider that runs entirely offline; it is labelled "Demo conversation" in the app. When a live server conversation provider is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app will only send text transcripts — never raw audio — to the server.

Crash and diagnostic data: V1 uses local logging only. No crash-reporting SDK is bundled in V1. If a crash reporter is added in a future version, this policy will be updated first.

2. Purchases

Subscriptions (SpeakLoop Pro, monthly or annual) are processed by Google Play. SpeakLoop does not receive or store your payment card details. Google Play may share your purchase status with the app so it can unlock Pro features and enforce the stated conversation-minute allowance. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles payment data.

3. What the app does NOT collect

  • No account, no email address, no name — V1 has no sign-up.
  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No contacts, photos, or files.
  • No advertising ID and no ads — the app contains no advertising SDKs.
  • No audio recordings. No conversation transcripts leave the device in V1.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): requested in context on the conversation screen only, so you can answer out loud. Optional — typing always works. Audio is processed on-device; see §1.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): requested only if you turn on lesson reminders in Settings.
  • Internet: used by Google Play Billing and by future server features. In V1, the conversation itself works offline.

5. Children

SpeakLoop is designed for adults (see the Play Store target-audience declaration). It is not directed at children under 13, and it knowingly collects no data from children.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app stores — word bank, streak, XP, progress, and settings — at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account & data. Deletion happens on your device immediately. Because V1 stores nothing on a server, there is nothing else to delete. To cancel a subscription, manage it in Google Play (Play Store → Subscriptions); cancelling stops future billing but does not delete your on-device learning data — use the in-app delete for that.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — most importantly, when a live server conversation provider is connected — this policy will be updated and the app will be updated to match before the change takes effect. The "Last updated" date above always reflects the current version.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: support@speakloop.app.
