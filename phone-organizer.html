Privacy Policy — Phone Organizer

Last updated: 21 November 2026  ·  App: Phone Organizer (com.phoneorganizer.app), version 0.11.0

Phone Organizer is a home-screen organizer: it arranges the apps already on your phone into tidy category pages, with a dock for your favorites. This policy explains what it does — and does not do — with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone. Nothing leaves the device.

1. What we collect

We collect nothing. Phone Organizer has no account system, no sign-in, no server, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK. There is no company server that receives your data, because there is no company server.

Your organization — which apps you place in which category, your dock choices, and your settings — is stored only in local app storage on your phone.

2. Your app list

To organize your home screen, the app needs to see which apps on your phone have a home-screen (launcher) entry. It asks Android for that list using the standard launcher visibility pattern only. That list is used on the device, to build your category pages — it is never transmitted anywhere. The app does not request the INTERNET permission and makes no network requests.

3. What leaves your phone

Nothing. The only outside service involved is Google Play itself, for downloading the app and any purchase you choose to make there. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy terms; the app never sees your payment details.

4. Permissions

Phone Organizer requests no Android permissions. It does not ask for location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone, or notification access.

5. Data deletion

All of the app's data lives in its local storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it, completely and immediately. There is no copy anywhere else to delete, because there is no copy anywhere else.

6. Children

Phone Organizer is a general-audience utility. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date before it takes effect.
